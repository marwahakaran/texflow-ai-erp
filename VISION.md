# Vision — TexFlow AI ERP

Product Mission
- Empower textile manufacturers and brands to streamline production, merchandising, and supply chain operations using an AI-first ERP designed for modern factories.

Product Vision
- Become the reference ERP for textile & apparel manufacturers by combining domain-focused workflows with AI-driven planning, automation, and analytics.

Core Principles
- Configuration over Customization
- Traceability and Auditability
- Modularity and Domain Ownership
- Security and Multi-tenant safety

Target Industries
- Apparel manufacturers
- Textile mills
- Contract manufacturers
- Vertical brands and merchandisers

AI Strategy
- Start with RAG-powered assistant and guided workflows for planning, forecasting, and exceptions.
- Move towards closed-loop automation: model suggests, human confirms, system executes.
- Use AI for demand forecasting, production planning, quality anomaly detection, and energy optimization.

Multi-company & Multi-factory Architecture
- Support multiple companies and factories in a single SaaS tenant with strict data partitioning per company and factory.

SaaS Readiness
- Configuration, onboarding flows, usage metering, tenant isolation, and upgrade paths designed from day one.

Mobile-first Roadmap
- Lightweight mobile apps for factory floor interactions: operator checklists, QC inspections, scanning, and simple approvals.

Global Expansion Strategy
- Support multi-currency, multi-language, and regional compliance modules.

Textile 4.0 / Smart Factory Vision
- Integrate IoT data streams (looms, dyeing machines) for real-time production telemetry and anomaly detection.

Digital Product Passport
- Maintain provenance metadata for each product and batch: composition, process history, certifications.

AI-driven Production Planning
- Use historical and real-time data to predict bottlenecks and optimize sequencing, resource allocation, and routing.

Predictive Analytics
- Predict quality issues, lead-time variability, and supplier risk using ML models and explainable insights.

Sustainability & Waste Reduction
- Track material usage, remnant stocks, and suggest remnant re-use or recycling plans to reduce waste.

Integration Strategy
- Provide adapters for accounting systems, WMS/TMS, e-commerce platforms, and IoT brokers. Use event-driven integration for decoupling.

Five-year product roadmap
- Year 1: Foundation (Authentication, Masters, Inventory, Merchandising)
- Year 2: Production workflows, Job work, Basic AI assistant
- Year 3: Advanced planning, Digital Product Passport, Supplier & Buyer portals
- Year 4: Smart factory integrations, mobile-first operations
- Year 5: Global scale SaaS, advanced predictive automation, marketplace integrations
